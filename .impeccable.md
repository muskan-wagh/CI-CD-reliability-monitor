## Design Context

### Users
SREs and engineers use Echo during CI triage and incident response to identify unreliable tests, inspect evidence, and take a focused next action.

### Brand Personality
Calm, forensic, operational.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, dense engineering console. The provided Echo reference is the visual direction: near-black surfaces, crisp borders, compact monospace metadata, restrained red and amber signal colors, and a persistent workspace shell.

### Design Principles
- Put evidence and next actions ahead of decoration.
- Use color sparingly and reserve it for reliability state.
- Keep navigation and context persistent across every page.
- Preserve dense information architecture without sacrificing keyboard access or readable contrast.
